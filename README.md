# Awesome AI Learning Notes

A curated collection of learning notes from worthwhile videos, podcasts, and courses about AI products, tools, and building independent software.

> **Status:** This collection is just getting started. No learning notes have been published yet.

## Why this collection exists

Many useful ideas are buried in long talks and conversations. This collection aims to help readers find worthwhile sources, understand their main ideas, and return to the original recording for context.

We value firsthand experience, clear explanations, practical depth, and enduring insights. New releases can be useful, but recency alone is not a reason to include something.

## Browse the collection

The first entries are being prepared. As the collection grows, notes will be organized by source type and topic:

- **Videos:** talks, demonstrations, and walkthroughs
- **Podcasts:** interviews and long-form conversations
- **Courses:** lectures, workshops, and learning series
- **Topics:** AI products, AI tools and workflows, and independent building

## What a note should contain

Each published note should identify the original source, creator, publication date, and preparation method. It should explain why the source is worth studying, provide concise original takeaways, and link important points to accurate timestamps when the source supports them. Notes should be checked against the source before publication; AI-generated text is a starting point for review, not a substitute for it.

Notes may be written by people or prepared with any suitable tool, including [ClipNoter](https://clipnoter.com/), which is built by the maintainer. No particular product is required to contribute or read this collection.

## Suggest or contribute

- Open an issue with the original URL, creator, and a short explanation of why the resource is useful.
- To contribute a note, open a pull request with source details, original takeaways, timestamps where available, and a description of how it was prepared and reviewed.
- Corrections are welcome. Please identify the source and the passage that needs attention.

## Attribution and rights

Link to the original work and credit its creators. Submit original summaries and avoid uploading full transcripts, recordings, paywalled materials, or other third-party content without the necessary rights. Referenced works remain subject to their respective rights and terms. Rights holders may open an issue to request a correction or removal.
